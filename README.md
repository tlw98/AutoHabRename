## What is AutoHabRename?

I realized I spent a significant amount of time naming stations, and that a very small amount of my time is spent actually building the stations themselves.
While templates exist, I find it's just as quick, and more reactive to simply quick build what you need. Templates for me at least, are not very useful for organization purposes.
This mod will automatically rename stations according to values in `modules.names.txt` and `orbit_names.txt`.

Adding a `!` at the start of a name will prevent that station from being renamed.

Note: All modules and orbits are not included in these lists (yet). I've included most major orbits and modules that have caps for interface orbits.

This mod was mostly made for myself, so in it's current state outside of anything breaking I consider it completed.
However I may iterate and add some more features eventually.
